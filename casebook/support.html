Casebook Support

Need help? Email ugenc19@ku.edu.tr and we'll get back to you within two working days.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a case. Tap Hint. It first corrects anyone you've placed in the wrong spot, then reveals where the most tightly pinned-down suspect stands. Every case has exactly one solution, and you never need to guess.

How do I restore purchases? Open Settings in the game (the gear at the top right) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel the Detective Club? In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Casebook. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. You keep the Club until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought coins but didn't get them. Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered on the next launch. If they still don't appear, email us.

Will I lose progress if I delete the game? Yes. Progress is stored only on your device. Purchases are kept with your Apple ID and come back with Restore purchases.
