Common questions

The game can't hear me. Make sure the microphone is allowed in iOS Settings › Echolings. Hold the Hold & Hum button the whole time you hum, and hum a little louder, close to the phone.

I keep missing the catch score. Tap Listen again and follow the shape of the yellow line: when it goes up, go up. Your key doesn't matter, only the shape and the timing. An Echo Amplifier lowers the score you need by 8.

How do I restore purchases? Open Settings in the game (the gear on the Explore screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel the Echo Pass? In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Echolings. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Is my voice recorded? No. It's analyzed on your phone in real time and never stored or uploaded. See the privacy policy.
