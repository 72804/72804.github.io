Echolings Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Echolings: Hum & Catch ("Echolings", "the app") is made by Ugur Genc ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Microphone and voice

Echolings uses your microphone only while you hold the Hold & Hum button. The audio is turned into pitch measurements on your device in real time and discarded right away. We never record, store or upload your voice, and no audio ever leaves your device.

Game data

Your progress (creatures caught, coins, streaks and settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. We do not run any servers that receive your game data.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of what you bought, but never your name, payment details or Apple ID.

Advertising

Echolings is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location;
  • your device model and operating system;
  • how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can turn off tracking any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying "Remove Ads" or the Echo Pass removes the ads that appear between games. Optional reward videos that you choose to watch still use AdMob.

Children

Echolings is suitable for all ages and requests only general-audience (G-rated) ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled by deleting the app or by changing your tracking and ad privacy settings. For requests about data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy. You can always contact us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
