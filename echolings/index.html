Echolings: Hum & Catch

Hum it back to catch it.

Thirty tiny creatures are hiding in meadows, caves, reefs, peaks and volcanoes. Each one sings its own call. Listen, then hold the button and hum, whistle or sing it back. Any voice, any key.

  • 30 Echolings across 5 zones, from common to legendary
  • A new Daily Call every day, the same tune for everyone. Keep your streak!
  • Pass-the-phone Party mode for 2 to 6 players
  • Your voice is analyzed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded
